# Cloud Physics at The University of Manchester

We develop numerical models, analysis tools and experimental software for research and teaching in **cloud physics, aerosol–cloud interactions and atmospheric science**.

Much of the software hosted here is **under active development** and is primarily intended for research and teaching. Individual repositories provide information on their current status, usage, licensing and validation where available.

## Research software

Our work includes:

### Cloud microphysics
Models for investigating aerosol activation, droplet growth, ice processes and cloud microphysical evolution.

### Cloud and atmospheric dynamics
Idealised and process-based models for studying cloud development and atmospheric dynamics.

### Measurement and instrumentation
Software and hardware supporting laboratory experiments and the analysis of atmospheric observations, including cloud-particle imaging.

### Teaching models
Numerical models used to explore atmospheric and cloud-physics concepts in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching.

## Using the software

Please check the individual repository before use. Development status, dependencies, licensing, validation and citation information may differ between projects.

If you are interested in using a model for research, please contact the relevant developer or open an issue in the appropriate repository.
